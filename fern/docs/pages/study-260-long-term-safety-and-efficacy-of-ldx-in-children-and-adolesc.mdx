## Long-term safety and efficacy of LDX in children and adolescents

**PMC** (2013)

**Authors:** Biederman et al.

**Country:** Europe

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Sustained efficacy over 2 years; dose optimization important; adverse events mild/moderate.
