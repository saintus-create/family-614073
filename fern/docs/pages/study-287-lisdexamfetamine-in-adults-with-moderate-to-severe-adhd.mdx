## Lisdexamfetamine in Adults with Moderate to Severe ADHD

**J Clin Psychopharm** (2008)

**Authors:** Adler et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Significant ADHD-RS-IV reduction; 70mg dose showed max efficacy in adults.
