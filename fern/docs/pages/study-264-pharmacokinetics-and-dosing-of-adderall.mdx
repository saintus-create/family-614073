**Authors:** Biederman et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 12.5–30 mg BID

## Key Findings

IR peaks at 2–3 hours; XR lasts ~12 hours; dose titration critical for therapeutic window.
