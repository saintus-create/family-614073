## Off-label prescription of psychopharmacological drugs for children and adolescents

**Ugeskrift for Laeger** (2017)

**Authors:** Pagsberg AK, Braüner JV, Høj Jensen H, Henriksen AB, Nielsen ES

**Region:** Denmark, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Psychopharmacological drugs (ADHD emphasis)

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label (estimated 30-40% rate)

## Key Findings

Confirmed that approximately 30-40% of prescriptions in Danish child psychiatry are off-label. ADHD medications are frequently prescribed to preschool children (off-label age).

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28874238/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28874238/)
