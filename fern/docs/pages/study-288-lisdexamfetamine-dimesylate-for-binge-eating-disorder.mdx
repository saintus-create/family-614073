## Lisdexamfetamine Dimesylate for Binge Eating Disorder

**JAMA Psychiatry** (2015)

**Authors:** McElroy et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Dose-dependent reduction in binge days; 50mg/70mg significantly superior to placebo.
