**PHARMACOLOGICAL TREATMENT · Formulation Evidence**

# Once-daily medications for the pharmacological management of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder in adults

> **KEY FINDINGS**
>
> Doses of 20, 40, and 60 mg MAS XR were effective and safe for adult ADHD, supporting the use of higher-than-typical daily units.

**CLINICAL QUESTION**
What is the efficacy and safety of once-daily mixed amphetamine salts up to 60 mg in adults?
