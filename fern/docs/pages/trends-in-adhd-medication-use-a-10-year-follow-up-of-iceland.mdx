## Trends in ADHD medication use: a 10-year follow-up of Icelandic children

**Journal of Child and Adolescent Psychopharmacology** (2012)

**Authors:** Zoëga H, Baldursson G, Hrafnkelsson B, Almarsdóttir AB, Valdimarsdóttir UA

**Region:** Iceland, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Preschool off-label use and dosing trends

## Key Findings

Found a dramatic increase in methylphenidate use in Iceland. It notes that a proportion of prescriptions were for preschool-aged children (off-label) and that multiple daily dosing was common for IR formulations.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22420412/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22420412/)
