## Drug therapy of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder

**Indian Journal of Psychiatry** (2012)

**Authors:** De Sousa A, Kalra G

**Region:** India

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine, TCAs, Clonidine

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label use of non-stimulants and antidepressants

## Key Findings

While methylphenidate is the gold standard, the study supports off-label use of atomoxetine, tricyclic antidepressants, and alpha-agonists (like clonidine) in Indian clinical settings, especially when stimulants are ineffective or poorly tolerated.
