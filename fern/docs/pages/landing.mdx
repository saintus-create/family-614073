# California's COHS Plans

## CenCal Health

Medi-Cal coverage · pharmacy · authorization · medical necessity · appeals

<Callout intent="info">
<strong>Start with the program.</strong> Medi-Cal is California's Medicaid program. Medicare is a separate federal health insurance program. For members in Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo Counties, CenCal Health is the local County Organized Health System (COHS) Medi-Cal managed care plan.
</Callout>

## Know which system applies

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Medi-Cal" icon="fa-regular fa-id-card">
    California's Medicaid program. This is not Medicare.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Medicare" icon="fa-regular fa-hospital-user">
    A separate federal health insurance program. Medicare rules do not automatically govern a Medi-Cal decision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Medi-Cal Rx" icon="fa-regular fa-prescription-bottle-medical">
    The statewide Medi-Cal pharmacy benefit for most outpatient prescription drugs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CenCal Health" icon="fa-regular fa-hospital">
    The COHS Medi-Cal managed care plan serving Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo Counties.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DHCS" icon="fa-regular fa-building-columns">
    California's Department of Health Care Services, the state agency that administers Medi-Cal.
  </Card>

## The path from prescription to decision

**Program** → Medi-Cal, Medicare, or both

**Benefit** → Pharmacy prescription or medical benefit

**Decision-maker** → Medi-Cal Rx, CenCal Health, or another responsible entity

**Authority** → Federal rule, California statute or regulation, DHCS requirement, contract, or authorized plan policy

**Review** → Coverage, prior authorization, utilization management, or medical necessity

**Deadline** → Required decision and notice timeframe

**Remedy** → Reconsideration, appeal, grievance, or State Fair Hearing, as applicable

<Callout intent="warning">
A plan policy is not treated as self-justifying. This handbook identifies the legal, regulatory, contractual, or delegated authority behind the requirement.
</Callout>
