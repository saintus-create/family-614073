## Reviews

#### [The World Federation of ADHD International Consensus Statement: 208 Evidence-based conclusions about the disorder](/pmid-33549739)

Faraone SV, et al. Neurosci Biobehav Rev. 2021 Sep. PMID: 33549739 · Review

Many findings in ADHD are supported by meta-analysis. These allow for firm statements about the nature, course, outcome causes, and treatments for disorders that are useful for reducing misconceptions and stigma.

#### [Cost of ADHD treatment using methylphenidate and atomoxetine in the South African private healthcare sector](/pmid-31456453)

#### [Lost in transition: A review of the unmet need of patients with attention deficit/hyperactivity disorder transitioning to adulthood](/pmid-27552649)

Treuer T, et al. Asia Pac Psychiatry. 2017 Jun. PMID: 27552649 · Review

The review suggests that the transition period to adult ADHD may be an underresearched and underserved area. The transition period plays an important role regarding how ADHD symptoms may be perceived and acted upon by adult psychiatrists.

#### [The implications of methylphenidate use by healthy medical students and doctors in South Africa](/pmid-24592964)

Beyer C, et al. BMC Med Ethics. 2014 Mar. PMID: 24592964 · Review

This article considers the impact of this use on the traditional role of medicine, society, the patient and suggests a way forward. We discuss the salient philosophy surrounding the use of cognitive enhancement.

## Other records

#### [Effect of Past Pharmacotherapy for Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder on Substance Use Disorder](/pmid-36349763)

Coetzee C, et al. Eur Addict Res. 2023. PMID: 36349763

Childhood ADHD pharmacotherapy might be associated with a decreased risk for substance use in adulthood and lower ADHD-related impairment. Despite study limitations, these findings underline the importance of early ADHD detection and treatment, which might prevent SUD.
