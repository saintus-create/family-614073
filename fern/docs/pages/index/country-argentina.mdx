## Evidence synthesis

#### [Cognitive-behavioural interventions for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in adults](/pmid-29566425)

Lopez PL, et al. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2018 Mar. PMID: 29566425 · Meta-analysis

Previous studies suggest that cognitive-behavioural therapy (CBT) could be effective in treating adults with ADHD, especially when combined with pharmacological treatment. CBT aims to change the thoughts and behaviours that reinforce harmful effects of the disorder by teaching people techniques to control the core symptoms.
