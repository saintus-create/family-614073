## Evidence synthesis

#### [Promoting Medication Adherence in Children with Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review with Meta-analysis and Qualitative Comparative Analysis](/pmid-38006238)

Thongseiratch T, et al. J Atten Disord. 2024 Jan. PMID: 38006238 · Meta-analysis

ADHD medication adherence interventions have a positive impact on both medication adherence and ADHD symptoms. Interventions should consider including ADHD drug education or tracking to maximize effectiveness.

#### [Exploratory meta-analysis on lisdexamfetamine versus placebo in adult ADHD](/pmid-25336914)

Maneeton N, et al. Drug Des Devel Ther. 2014. PMID: 25336914 · Meta-analysis

The number of included studies was limited (five RCTs), but based on this meta-analysis, LDX is efficacious and well tolerated in the treatment of adult ADHD. Additionally, it also improved the executive function deficits in this population. However, its acceptability is no higher than placebo. These findings should be carefully interpreted and considered as preliminary outcomes. To confirm these results, further studies are warranted. LDX is a viable alternative psychostimulant for adult ADHD.

## Trials

#### [Effect of Iron Supplementation in Children with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder and Iron Deficiency: A Randomized Controlled Trial](/pmid-34313619)

Pongpitakdamrong A, et al. J Dev Behav Pediatr. 2022. PMID: 34313619 · Randomized controlled trial

Children with ADHD and iron deficiency being on methylphenidate and iron supplementation had shown improvement of ADHD symptoms that were reported by parents.

## Observational studies

#### [Remote monitoring of wide-ranging real-world changes in adults following ADHD medication initiation](/pmid-41298359)

Zhang Y, et al. Transl Psychiatry. 2025 Nov. PMID: 41298359 · Cohort study

These findings suggest ADHD medication in adults is associated with broad real-world benefits alongside some potential concerns, such as elevated heart rate and delayed sleep. This study highlights the potential of remote monitoring technologies to capture multidimensional treatment responses in daily life and provides a foundation for future research to support both clinical care and self-management in ADHD.
