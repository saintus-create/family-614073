## Evidence synthesis

#### [Long-Term Efficacy of Methylphenidate Immediate-Release for the Treatment of Childhood ADHD](/pmid-25501355)

Maia CR, et al. J Atten Disord. 2017 Jan. PMID: 25501355 · Meta-analysis

MPH-IR is efficacious for childhood ADHD for periods longer than 12 weeks.

#### [Immediate-release methylphenidate for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in adults](/pmid-25230710)

Epstein T, et al. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2014 Sep. PMID: 25230710 · Meta-analysis

Although the usefulness of immediate-release methylphenidate in children has been extensively studied, studies in adults, which are few, demonstrate varying results.

#### [Extended-release methylphenidate for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in adults](/pmid-35201607)

Boesen K, et al. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2022 Feb. PMID: 35201607 · Systematic review

Extended-release methylphenidate versus placebo (up to 26 weeks) For the primary outcomes, we found very low-certainty evidence that methylphenidate had no effect on 'days missed at work' at 13-week follow-up (mean difference (MD) -0.

Hypotheses can be generated relating to neuroadaptive changes, to explain these findings. Atomoxetine has a trajectory of response that is incremental over a long period of time, with a greater than expected maintenance of response.

#### [An evaluation of the pharmacokinetics of methylphenidate for the treatment of attention-deficit/ hyperactivity disorder](/pmid-24856438)

Frölich J, et al. Expert Opin Drug Metab Toxicol. 2014 Aug. PMID: 24856438 · Review

Controlled studies have demonstrated an effective reduction in the core symptoms of the disorder following MPH therapy, although long-term studies also demonstrate that the therapeutic benefits dissipate in the absence of combined psychosocial interventions.

#### [Safety of medicines used for ADHD in children: a review of published prospective clinical trials](/pmid-24748641)

Clavenna A, et al. Arch Dis Child. 2014 Sep. PMID: 24748641 · Review

Few studies evaluated the long-term safety of drugs for ADHD. Heterogeneity in follow-up duration and in data reporting made comparing different studies and drugs difficult. A systematic monitoring of long-term safety is needed.

#### [Guanfacine extended-release for attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)](/pmid-20831361)

Sallee FR, et al. Expert Opin Pharmacother. 2010 Oct. PMID: 20831361 · Review

Study evidence indicates that GXR is a well-tolerated and effective treatment for children and adolescents with ADHD, and appears efficacious to reduce oppositional symptoms in children with these complicating features. The GXR safety database reflects mild and asymptomatic decreases in both blood pressure and heart rate throughout, with most adverse events being somnolence-related and time-limited.

#### [Evolution of stimulants to treat ADHD: transdermal methylphenidate](/pmid-19051222)

Patrick KS, et al. Hum Psychopharmacol. 2009 Jan. PMID: 19051222 · Review

While liquid or chewable MPH formulations overcome potential swallowing difficulties, as do sprinkled once-daily extended-release (ER) MPH products, only MTS addresses swallowing difficulties while also offering a flexible individualized MPH exposure time in a once-daily MPH regimen.
