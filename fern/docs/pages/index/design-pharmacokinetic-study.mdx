#### [l-Arginine/nitric oxide pathway and oxidative stress in adults with ADHD: Effects of methylphenidate treatment](/pmid-37392928)

Sinningen K, et al. Nitric Oxide. 2023 Sep. PMID: 37392928 · Pharmacokinetic study

Adult patients with ADHD, who are not treated with MPH (-MPH), showed varied Arg/NO pathway, but Arg bioavailability seemed to be consistent over the groups. Our findings imply that urinary reabsorption may be increase and/or excretion of nitrite and nitrate may be decreased in ADHD, resulting in an increase in the plasma concentration of nitrite. MPH seems to partially reverse these effects by not yet known mechanisms, and does not affect oxidative stress.

Lan T, et al. Eur J Drug Metab Pharmacokinet. 2017 Apr. PMID: 27207674 · Pharmacokinetic study

Our study showed that myricetin could significantly inhibit the formation of atomoxetine metabolite both in vivo and in vitro. It is recommended that the effect of myricetin on the metabolism of atomoxetine should be noted and atomoxetine plasma concentration should be monitored.
