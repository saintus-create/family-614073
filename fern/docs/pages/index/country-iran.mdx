## Evidence synthesis

#### [Comparative efficacy and tolerability of medications for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder in children, adolescents, and adults: a systematic review and network meta-analysis](/pmid-30097390)

Cortese S, et al. Lancet Psychiatry. 2018 Sep. PMID: 30097390 · Systematic review

Our findings represent the most comprehensive available evidence base to inform patients, families, clinicians, guideline developers, and policymakers on the choice of ADHD medications across age groups. Taking into account both efficacy and safety, evidence from this meta-analysis supports methylphenidate in children and adolescents, and amphetamines in adults, as preferred first-choice medications for the short-term treatment of ADHD. New research should be funded urgently to assess long-term effects of these drugs.

## Trials

#### [Sulforaphane Adjunct to Methylphenidate for Attention-deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Trial](/pmid-41604557)

Ghannadi F, et al. Clin Neuropharmacol. 2025. PMID: 41604557 · Randomized controlled trial

Sulforaphane adjunct to methylphenidate was beneficial for inattention, hyperactivity-impulsivity, and total symptoms of children with ADHD safely and tolerably.

#### [Crocus sativus L. Versus Methylphenidate in Treatment of Children with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Randomized, Double-Blind Pilot Study](/pmid-30741567)

Baziar S, et al. J Child Adolesc Psychopharmacol. 2019 Apr. PMID: 30741567 · Randomized controlled trial

Short-term therapy with saffron capsule showed the same efficacy compared with methylphenidate. Nevertheless, larger controlled studies with longer treatment periods are necessary for future studies.

## Observational studies

#### [Prevalence of elimination disorders and comorbid psychiatric disorders in Iranian children and adolescents](/pmid-33720855)

Mohammadi MR, et al. J Pediatr Rehabil Med. 2021. PMID: 33720855 · Cross-sectional study

The prevalence of Elimination Disorders in Iranian children and adolescents is moderate compared to similar studies elsewhere. As for comorbid disorders, ADHD and Separation Anxiety were found to be the most prevalent disorders. Since Elimination Disorders coexist with psychiatric disorders in children, further studies of these comorbidities may give better insight into the treatment and prognosis of Elimination Disorders.

## Reviews

#### [Cannabinoids and ADHD: a New Frontier in Neuropharmacology?](/pmid-41165995)

Mavaddat H, et al. J Mol Neurosci. 2025 Oct. PMID: 41165995 · Review

Existing data indicate that individuals with ADHD are at a higher risk of substance use, and some may develop cannabis dependence or engage in self-medication with it. It has been hypothesized that individuals with ADHD may utilize cannabis to mitigate certain symptoms, such as anxiety, or to alleviate the adverse effects of their prescribed medication.
