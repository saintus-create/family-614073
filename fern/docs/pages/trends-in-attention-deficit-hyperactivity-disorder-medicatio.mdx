## Trends in attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder medication use: a retrospective observational study using population-based databases

**The Lancet Psychiatry** (2018)

**Authors:** Raman SR, Man KKC, Bahmanyar S, Berard A, et al.

**Region:** Northern Europe (UK, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland), Canada

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Amphetamines, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Global trends including off-label adult prescribing

## Key Findings

The study found significant increases in ADHD medication prescribing for adults across Northern Europe and Canada, despite many medications not having initial adult labels, indicating widespread off-label adult use.
