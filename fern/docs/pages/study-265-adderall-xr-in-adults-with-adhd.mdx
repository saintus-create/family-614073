## Adderall XR in adults with ADHD

**J Clin Psychiatry** (2001)

**Authors:** Spencer et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 20 mg once (XR)

## Key Findings

Approved dose up to 20 mg/day; higher doses not supported by superior benefits.
