## Prescription Drug Prior Authorization or Step Therapy Exception Request Form (No. 61-211)

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Issuer | CA Department of Managed Health Care / Department of Insurance |
| Authority | 28 CCR 1300.67.241(a); Health & Safety Code 1367.241(c)-(d) |
| Revision | Revised 12/2016 |

> Plans must use and accept only this form. It may not exceed two pages, and plans must make it electronically available.
