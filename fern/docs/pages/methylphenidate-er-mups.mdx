## Safety and effectiveness of methylphenidate ER multi-unit pellet system in ADHD patients: An open label study

**South African Journal of Psychiatry** (2024)

**Authors:** Renata Schoeman

**Medication:** Methylphenidate ER (Multi-unit pellet system)

**Usage:** Multi-unit delivery system (ER pellets)

**Study Type:** Open-Label Study

**Country:** South Africa

## Key Findings

The study confirmed the safety and efficacy of the multi-unit particulate system (MUPS) in improving ADHD symptoms in adult and pediatric populations.

## Argument Support

Investigates the safety and effectiveness of a multi-unit pellet system for extended release, supporting more complex delivery formats for ADHD management.
