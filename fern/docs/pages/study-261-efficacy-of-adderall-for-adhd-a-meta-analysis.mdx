## Efficacy of Adderall for ADHD: a meta-analysis

**J Atten Disord** (2002)

**Authors:** Faraone & Biederman

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 12.5–30 mg BID

## Key Findings

Significant improvement in inattention/aggression; pooled SMD 1.00 (95% CI: 0.91, 1.10).

**DOI/Link:** NBK69423
