## Patterns of lisdexamfetamine use in Europe

**PMC** (2021)

**Authors:** Cortese et al.

**Country:** Europe

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

67.8% repeat users; mean daily dose 51.08 mg; long-term exposure (555+ days) effective.

**DOI/Link:** PMC7475084
