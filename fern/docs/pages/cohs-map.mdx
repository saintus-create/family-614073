## Why a plan map belongs in the responsibility index

A member or provider may begin with a county, a benefit, or a plan logo rather than a policy title. The map provides a visual first step: identify the COHS plan associated with the service area, then move into the jurisdiction, obligation, and escalation layers.

The map distinguishes plan identity from benefit ownership. For example, a prescription pharmacy transaction may route through Medi-Cal Rx even when the member is enrolled in a COHS plan, while a physician-administered drug may route through the plan's medical-pharmacy workflow.

## Map implementation

The Fern landing page already uses Mapbox GL JS for its California COHS map. The canonical map layer now adds six interactive plan-logo markers to that existing instance, each linked to the plan's official site and labeled with its service-area identity.

The implementation follows Mapbox's custom-marker pattern: each plan is represented by a keyboard-accessible DOM marker with a local image, a plan record, coordinates, and a linked official source. Plan identity assets are stored under `fern/assets/cohs-logos/`, with a text fallback if an image cannot be loaded.

## Source and verification boundary

The six-plan model is based on the Local Health Plans of California description of six COHS plans operating in 22 counties and the DHCS Medi-Cal Managed Care Health Plan Directory. The map is an orientation aid, not a legal county-boundary or eligibility determination. Confirm the current county directory, plan enrollment rules, benefit channel, effective date, and responsible administrator before using it for a member-specific matter.
