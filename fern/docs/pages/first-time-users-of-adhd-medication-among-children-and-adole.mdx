**Journal:** Frontiers in Psychiatry (2021)

**Authors:** Oliver Scholle, Kai Kollhorst, Edeltraut Riedel

**Medication:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine, Lisdexamfetamine

**Usage Type:** Off-label (outside age or indication guidelines)

**Study Type:** Pharmacoepidemiological Study / Claims Data Analysis

**Country:** Germany

**DOI:** [10.3389/fpsyt.2021.653093](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2021.653093)

## Key Findings

Found that approximately 42.5% of first-time users in Germany received medication that was off-label in terms of drug choice or patient age.

## Argument Support

Provides evidence of the widespread clinical practice of off-label prescribing, reflecting clinical support for non-standard use in children and adolescents.
