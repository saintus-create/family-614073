<Callout intent="info" title="Build facts before conclusions">
The checklist below is a documentation framework. It does not determine medical necessity or guarantee authorization.
</Callout>

## Request identity

<Steps>
<Step title="Medication and proposed use">
Record generic and brand name when relevant, formulation/strength, route, requested dose, quantity, frequency, duration, diagnosis, indication, and patient population. State whether the proposed use differs from the current US FDA labeling.
</Step>
<Step title="Benefit channel">
Record whether the request is pharmacy-dispensed or physician-administered/billed through the medical benefit. For the current CenCal source set, pharmacy-benefit PA is routed through Medi-Cal Rx; medical PAD/TAR processing remains a CenCal workflow.
</Step>
<Step title="Coverage status">
Record the current formulary/preferred status or PAD/code requirement if verified. If the repository does not establish the current status, mark it **verification required** rather than assuming non-preferred or covered status.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Clinical justification checklist

- [ ] Diagnosis and clinical indication are documented.
- [ ] Exact requested regimen is stated.
- [ ] Patient-specific medical necessity is explained.
- [ ] Prior therapies and outcomes are documented.
- [ ] Covered/preferred alternatives are identified and the reason they are unsuitable, failed, contraindicated, or expected to be ineffective is documented when applicable.
- [ ] Relevant chart notes and objective findings are attached where relevant.
- [ ] Evidence supporting the proposed use is cited.
- [ ] Material limitations or contradictory evidence are acknowledged.
- [ ] Monitoring and follow-up plan are documented where clinically relevant.

## Submission checklist

For a pharmacy-benefit request, verify the current Medi-Cal Rx PA route and form revision before submission. The repository's source-linked record identifies DHCS 6560 as the PA form and also identifies electronic submission routes.

For a CenCal physician-administered drug, verify the current PAD List/code requirements and use the applicable CenCal TAR route. A downloadable/fax route may exist when portal submission is not possible; verify the current instructions before sending.

<Callout intent="warning" title="Form revisions matter">
A linked form is not proof that it is the current revision or that it applies to every drug. Record the form name, revision/effective date when available, source URL, and the date you verified it.
</Callout>

## Packet provenance

For each material attachment or proposition, capture:

- source authority
- URL or document identifier
- jurisdiction and plan
- effective/publication date
- verification date
- whether it is primary authority, agency/plan guidance, clinical evidence, or a real-world decision record

This keeps an evidence packet auditable without turning source material into an unsupported legal or clinical conclusion.
