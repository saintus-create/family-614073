## The routing decision

<Steps>
<Step title="Classify the medication request">
Record medication, formulation, proposed use, dose, route, quantity, frequency, duration, diagnosis, and patient population. Compare the proposed use with the current FDA label so approved-label and off-label status are explicit.
</Step>

<Step title="Identify the benefit channel">
If the medicine is dispensed through a pharmacy, route the request through Medi-Cal Rx. If it is physician-administered and billed through the medical benefit, check the applicable CenCal PAD/TAR workflow. Do not infer the responsible reviewer from the drug name alone.
</Step>

<Step title="Build the authorization packet">
Assemble the applicable PA/TAR form or electronic route, clinical records, prior treatment and alternatives, requested regimen, medical-necessity explanation, and evidence supporting the proposed use. Mark expedited requests only when the applicable urgent standard is met.
</Step>

<Step title="Track the decision state">
Record whether the request was approved, partially approved, modified, deferred/pend, denied, or returned for information. Preserve the written reason and criteria used rather than paraphrasing it into an unsupported conclusion.
</Step>

<Step title="Route the next step">
An approval proceeds under its conditions. A modification, deferral, or denial moves into the applicable reconsideration or appeal pathway. For Medi-Cal managed care, distinguish the plan appeal process from State Fair Hearing and do not assume commercial-plan IMR rules apply.
</Step>
</Steps>
