<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Member">
    **Primary job:** understand the request and the decision.

    Ask which entity made the decision, request the written Notice of Action when applicable, track the appeal deadline, and identify whether the next route is plan appeal, State Fair Hearing, or another program-specific process.

    **Do not infer:** that a denial means the treatment is clinically inappropriate, or that a commercial-plan IMR process automatically applies.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Prescriber / provider">
    **Primary job:** create a complete, patient-specific authorization record.

    Document the indication, exact regimen, medical necessity, alternatives, prior treatment, supporting and contradictory evidence, monitoring plan, and the correct pharmacy-versus-medical submission route.

    **Do not infer:** that an off-label prescription is automatically covered or automatically prohibited.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pharmacist">
    **Primary job:** route the dispensing request through the correct pharmacy-benefit process and reconcile the prescription with the applicable authorization status.

    For the current CenCal source record, pharmacy benefit processing is administered through Medi-Cal Rx. Confirm the current formulary, PA requirement, submission route, and response before dispensing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Plan reviewer / staff">
    **Primary job:** preserve the request facts, applicable criteria, decision state, notices, and provenance.

    Record whether the request is approved, partially approved, modified, deferred, denied, or awaiting information. Where a denial or modification relies on criteria, preserve the criteria and source used for the case.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Legal / compliance">
    **Primary job:** test scope, authority, effective date, and provenance.

    Distinguish statute/regulation from agency guidance, plan policy, clinical evidence, and a real-world decision. Check whether a proposition actually applies to the county, plan, benefit channel, medication, and date at issue.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Shared handoff

<Steps>
<Step title="Facts">
All roles should be able to see the medication, formulation, requested use, diagnosis, regimen, benefit channel, and relevant dates.
</Step>
<Step title="Evidence and authority">
The record should expose the supporting sources and material limitations without presenting a literature citation as a guarantee of coverage.
</Step>
<Step title="Decision">
The written determination and its stated reason should remain attached to the request.
</Step>
<Step title="Next action">
The next action should be tied to the actual program and notice, including any applicable deadline or expedited route.
</Step>
</Steps>
