<Callout intent="info" title="A decision state is not a clinical judgment">
The status recorded by an authorization system describes an administrative decision. It should be preserved with the written reason, criteria, scope, date, and next available process.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Approved">
    Record the approved drug, formulation, dose/quantity limits, duration, effective period, conditions, and any remaining dispensing or administration requirements. Approval does not replace the prescription or clinical judgment.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Partially approved">
    Compare the requested and approved regimen. Record exactly what changed and whether the remaining portion requires a new request, clarification, or appeal.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Modified">
    Preserve the requested regimen, the modified regimen, the stated reason, and the criteria used. Do not describe the modification as a finding that the original treatment was medically inappropriate unless the decision actually says so.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deferred / pended">
    Treat a pend as an information or review state. Record the missing information, request date, response deadline if stated, and who must respond. A pend is not the same as a denial.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Denied">
    Preserve the written denial, reason, criteria, notice date, benefit channel, and appeal deadline. Identify whether the issue is medical necessity, benefit exclusion, formulary/PA criteria, missing information, coding, or another stated basis without inventing a reason.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Information requested">
    Track the exact records requested, source of the request, date received, response route, and whether the request changes the applicable review timeframe.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Minimum decision record

<Steps>
<Step title="Identify the decision maker">
Record CenCal, Medi-Cal Rx/DHCS, or another responsible entity and the benefit channel that gave rise to the decision.
</Step>
<Step title="Capture the written decision">
Store the determination/Notice of Action and the stated rationale. Preserve the exact criteria or policy identified as controlling when available.
</Step>
<Step title="Capture dates and deadlines">
Record request date, decision date, notice date, authorization period, response deadline, and appeal deadline when stated. Do not substitute a generic deadline for a missing source-specific date.
</Step>
<Step title="Route the next action">
Approval proceeds under its conditions. Modification, deferral, denial, or unresolved requests move to the applicable clarification, reconsideration, appeal, or other review route.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout intent="warning" title="Do not flatten benefit channels">
The repository's CenCal source record distinguishes pharmacy-benefit PA administered through Medi-Cal Rx from CenCal medical-benefit PAD/TAR processing. The appeal and submission path must follow the actual benefit channel.
</Callout>
