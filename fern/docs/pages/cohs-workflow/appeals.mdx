<Callout intent="warning" title="First identify the program and benefit channel">
A Medi-Cal managed-care appeal is not automatically the same thing as a commercial-plan grievance, DMHC IMR, or pharmacy-benefit appeal. Confirm the entity that issued the decision and use the notice and current program rules as the controlling starting point.
</Callout>

## Route after a denial or modification

<Steps>
<Step title="Preserve the determination">
Collect the determination or Notice of Action, decision date, stated reason, cited criteria, requested and approved/denied service, and the applicable benefit channel.
</Step>
<Step title="Check for clarification or reconsideration">
If the decision identifies missing information or a reviewer contact, use that route when available. For a CenCal TAR, the source record identifies physician-reviewer contact and a TAR Appeal Form for denied, deferred, or modified decisions.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the appeal deadline">
For the source-linked CenCal provider TAR pathway, the recorded appeal window is 90 calendar days from the original decision. For a Medi-Cal member appeal, the recorded source says the member generally has 60 calendar days from the Notice of Action. Verify the current notice and governing rule before relying on either deadline.
</Step>
<Step title="Determine who may file">
A provider may file a provider TAR appeal where the plan route permits it. In the Medi-Cal member appeal system, a provider acting for the member may need the member's written consent. Confirm representation requirements for the actual proceeding.
</Step>
<Step title="Escalate only to the applicable next forum">
For Medi-Cal managed care, the source record identifies the MCP appeal followed, where applicable, by a DHCS State Fair Hearing. Do not automatically route a CenCal Medi-Cal dispute to the ordinary commercial-plan DMHC IMR process.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What to include in an appeal packet

- [ ] Original request and requested regimen.
- [ ] Determination / Notice of Action.
- [ ] Exact reason and criteria stated by the decision maker.
- [ ] Clinical records addressing the stated reason.
- [ ] Prior treatment and alternative history where relevant.
- [ ] Evidence supporting the requested use, including limitations.
- [ ] Correct current form or electronic submission route.
- [ ] Proof of authorization/representation where required.
- [ ] A clear requested resolution: approve, restore, correct, or reconsider the specified service.
- [ ] Source, date, jurisdiction, plan, and verification metadata for material authority.

<Callout intent="note" title="Urgent review">
The repository's source record identifies expedited authorization and expedited appeal pathways with shorter timeframes. Use an expedited route only when the applicable program's urgent standard is satisfied and document the clinical risk of delay.
</Callout>

## State Fair Hearing and IMR boundary

The current source record describes a DHCS State Fair Hearing pathway after the Medi-Cal managed-care appeal process and separately flags ordinary DMHC IMR as a jurisdiction question for this Medi-Cal COHS context. Treat that distinction as a routing safeguard, not as a universal legal rule. Current eligibility and exhaustion requirements must be verified against the governing program and notice.
