<Callout intent="info" title="Completeness is scoped, not absolute">
“Complete” means complete for the stated jurisdiction, plan, benefit channel, medication scope, and effective date. The bank does not claim that every California law, COHS, formulary, form, or medication is represented.
</Callout>

## Provenance states

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Verified">
    The record may be presented as verified against its designated source, subject to the source's scope and effective date.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Source-derived">
    The fact is reproduced or structured from a source but should not be presented as independently verified current law or policy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Review required">
    The source helps locate an issue, but a reviewer should confirm the current authority, applicability, or version before relying on it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Unreviewed">
    Discovery-only material. It should not be used as an authoritative proposition.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Before relying on a workflow

<Steps>
<Step title="Match scope">
Confirm county, plan/program, benefit channel, medication/formulation, requested use, and date.
</Step>
<Step title="Match authority">
Open the underlying statute, regulation, agency/plan policy, contract provision, form, or source record rather than relying on a summary alone.
</Step>
<Step title="Check currentness">
Confirm the source is current for the date of the request and record the verification date.
</Step>
<Step title="Record the gap">
If the bank does not establish an answer, label it **verification required**. Do not convert absence of data into a negative coverage or legal conclusion.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout intent="warning" title="No universal-coverage claim">
The completeness dashboard is deliberately conservative. A missing formulary entry, plan policy, form revision, or jurisdiction-specific rule means the answer is unknown from this bank—not that the item does not exist.
</Callout>
