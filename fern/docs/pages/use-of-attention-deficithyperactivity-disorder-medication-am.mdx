## Use of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder medication among adults aged >=50 years in Denmark

**European Journal of Clinical Pharmacology** (2018)

**Authors:** Ormhøj SS, Pottegård A, Hallas J, Sahlholdt CP, Nielsen ES

**Region:** Denmark, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine, Lisdexamfetamine

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label use in older adults (age >= 50)

## Key Findings

Observed a 10-fold increase in ADHD medication use among adults aged 50+ years between 2005 and 2015, highlighting a shift towards off-label age group prescribing as diagnosis in adults grows.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29493809/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29493809/)
