## Off-label prescribing of psychotropic drugs in a Danish child and adolescent psychiatric outpatient clinic

**Basic & Clinical Pharmacology & Toxicology** (2016)

**Authors:** Nielsen ES, Henriksen AB, Jensen HS, Olsen MH, Hansen MB

**Region:** Denmark, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Psychotropic drugs (including ADHD medications like methylphenidate)

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label use regarding age and indication

## Key Findings

Found that 32.7% of patients received at least one off-label prescription. Methylphenidate was among the most common off-label drugs, often prescribed to children outside the approved age range.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25724547/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25724547/)
