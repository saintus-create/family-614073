## Once-daily medications for the pharmacological management of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder in adults

**Clinical Psychopharmacology and Neuroscience (or Clinical Medicine Insights: Psychiatry)** (2009)

**Authors:** OV Tcheremissine

**Medication:** Mixed Amphetamine Salts (MAS XR)

**Usage:** Off-label (High-dose up to 60mg/day in adults)

**Study Type:** Review / Clinical Trial Summary

**Country:** USA

## Key Findings

Doses of 20, 40, and 60 mg MAS XR were effective and safe for adult ADHD, supporting the use of higher-than-typical daily units.

## Argument Support

Discusses the use of doses up to 60mg, which was off-label for many adult patients at the time, supporting higher-dose regimens.
