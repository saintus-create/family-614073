## A Review of Long-Acting Medications for ADHD in Canada

**Journal of the Canadian Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry** (2009)

**Authors:** Hosenbocus S, Chahal R

**Region:** Canada

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate ER, Mixed Amphetamine Salts XR, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Multiple doses per day (IR) vs Extended-release

## Key Findings

Discusses that before the availability of long-acting agents, multiple daily doses of immediate-release medications were the standard. It supports the clinical practice of using IR boosters to manage 'rebound' or cover evening activities.

**Source:** [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2765387/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2765387/)
