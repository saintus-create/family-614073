## Treatment Outcomes With Licensed and Unlicensed Stimulant Doses for Adults With Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**JAMA Psychiatry** (2024)

**Authors:** Luis C. Farhat, José M. Flores, Samuele Cortese, Michael H. Bloch

**Medication:** Methylphenidate, Amphetamines

**Usage:** Off-label (unlicensed high-dose)

**Study Type:** Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Country:** Global (Meta-Analysis)

## Key Findings

Meta-analysis found that unlicensed stimulant doses were not associated with greater efficacy than licensed high doses but had a significantly higher risk of discontinuation due to adverse events. However, it provides the safety profile for such off-label usage.

## Argument Support

The study explicitly investigates the efficacy and safety of unlicensed stimulant doses (doses exceeding regulatory guidelines) compared to licensed doses in adults.
