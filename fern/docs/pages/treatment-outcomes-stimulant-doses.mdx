## Key Findings

Meta-analysis found that unlicensed stimulant doses were not associated with greater efficacy than licensed high doses but had a significantly higher risk of discontinuation due to adverse events. However, it provides the safety profile for such off-label usage.

## Clinical Question

Are unlicensed (high) stimulant doses more effective than licensed doses in adults, and at what safety cost?
