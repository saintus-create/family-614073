## Potential Role of Dexamphetamine in the Treatment of Non-ADHD Conditions

**Journal of Postgraduate Medicine, Education and Research** (2021)

**Authors:** Gautam CS, Saha L, Grewal H

**Region:** India

**Medication focus:** Dexamphetamine

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label drug for various conditions

## Key Findings

Highlights that dexamphetamine, primarily an ADHD drug, is used off-label in India for conditions like refractory depression and fatigue, showing the flexibility of ADHD stimulants in clinical practice.
