## The Use of Medication Against Attention deficit/hyperactivity Disorder in Denmark: A Drug Use Study From a Patient Perspective

**European Journal of Clinical Pharmacology** (2013)

**Authors:** Pottegård A, Bjerregaard BK, Glumer C, Hallas J, Nielsen ES

**Region:** Denmark, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label users (age < 6 and age > 17)

## Key Findings

Dropout rates were highest among off-label users. However, the study confirms that off-label use (under 6 and adults) is a significant part of the prescribing landscape in Denmark.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22811260/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22811260/)
