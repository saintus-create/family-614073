## Effect of Food on LDX Pharmacokinetics

**Clin Pharmacokinet** (2008)

**Authors:** Krishnan et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

High-fat meal does not alter AUC or Cmax; Tmax delayed by only 1 hour.
