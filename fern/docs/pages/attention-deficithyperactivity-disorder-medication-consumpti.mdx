## Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder medication consumption in 64 countries and regions, 2015–2019: a longitudinal study

**eClinicalMedicine** (2023)

**Authors:** Chan AYL, et al.

**Region:** Northern Europe, Canada, India

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Lisdexamfetamine, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Trends in consumption and off-label usage

## Key Findings

Reported an 18.08% annual increase in ADHD medication consumption in Northern Europe. It notes that while labels vary, clinical use often involves off-label indications for adults and comorbid management.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36618538/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36618538/)
