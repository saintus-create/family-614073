<Callout intent="info">
**Start with facts, not conclusions.** This workspace organizes the clinical record and applicable authority. It does not decide whether a prescription is appropriate.
</Callout>

## Build the record in the right order

<Steps toc={true}>

<Step title="Define the proposed use">
Record the drug, formulation, dose, route, diagnosis, patient population, and proposed indication. Compare the proposed use with the current FDA labeling.

[Compare FDA labeling](/regulatory)
</Step>

<Step title="Determine which California rules apply">
Start with the structured authority registry. The system distinguishes mandatory requirements from permissive authority, advisory guidance, and evidence that supports a clinical proposition.

[California authority registry](/california-off-label/authority-registry)
</Step>

<Step title="Build the evidence dossier">
Use the existing evidence index, clinical trials, guidelines, pharmacokinetic material, safety evidence, and off-label literature. Record both supporting and material contradictory evidence.

[Evidence framework](/california-off-label/evidence)
</Step>

<Step title="Document the clinical reasoning">
State why the proposed use is medically indicated for this patient, what alternatives were considered, what is known about benefits and risks, and how the response will be monitored.

[Documentation framework](/california-off-label/documentation)
</Step>

<Step title="Address informed discussion and consent">
Determine what disclosure is required by the applicable authority and what additional discussion is clinically appropriate. Keep statutory, setting-specific, drug-specific, and general consent rules distinct.

[Consent framework](/california-off-label/consent)
</Step>

<Step title="Check additional requirements">
Screen for controlled-substance, telehealth, workers' compensation, facility, public-program, and payer requirements before finalizing the record.

[Additional requirements](/california-off-label/additional-requirements)
</Step>

<Step title="Separate prescribing from coverage">
If authorization or reimbursement is at issue, use the coverage pathway separately. A payer decision is not itself a determination of whether the clinician may prescribe.

[Coverage authorization](/coverage)
</Step>

</Steps>

## What the completed record should contain

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Authority" icon="fa-regular fa-scale-balanced" href="/california-off-label/authority-registry">Applicable statutes, regulations, guidance, effective dates, and obligation type.</Card>
  <Card title="Evidence" icon="fa-regular fa-flask" href="/california-off-label/evidence">Supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, quality, relevance, and provenance.</Card>
  <Card title="Clinical record" icon="fa-regular fa-file-medical" href="/california-off-label/documentation">Patient-specific indication, rationale, alternatives, consent, monitoring, and follow-up.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## The decision path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Prescribing">
    **Can this clinician prescribe this drug for this patient and proposed use?** Establish authority, examination, medical indication, standard of care, and any drug- or setting-specific constraints.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Evidence">
    **What supports the proposed use?** Assemble the strongest relevant evidence and explicitly record material limitations or contradictory findings.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Coverage">
    **Will a payer or program authorize it?** Apply the separate coverage rules and evidence standards after the clinical record is established.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Evidence dossier

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Supports">Studies, guidelines, systematic reviews, regulatory material, and other evidence supporting efficacy or safety.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Contradicts or limits">Negative studies, safety signals, population limitations, uncertainty, conflicting guidelines, and evidence that weakens the rationale.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Patient-specific">Facts that make the evidence applicable, inapplicable, stronger, weaker, or otherwise relevant to this patient.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authority provenance">Exact statute, regulation, agency material, FDA source, guideline, and evidence citation with applicability and verification metadata.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Callout intent="note">
The target output is an auditable chain: **proposed use → applicable authority → clinical rationale → evidence → consent → documentation → monitoring → coverage or appeal**.
</Callout>
