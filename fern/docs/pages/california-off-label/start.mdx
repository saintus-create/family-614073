<Callout intent="info">
This is the provider-facing starting point. It does not decide whether a prescription is appropriate. It organizes the facts, authorities, evidence, and documentation that must be reviewed.
</Callout>

## Start with the clinical facts

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Drug & formulation" icon="fa-regular fa-pills" href="/regulatory">
    Identify the exact active ingredient, formulation, route, strength, and current FDA labeling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patient & condition" icon="fa-regular fa-user-doctor" href="/california-off-label/prescribing-authority">
    Record the patient population, diagnosis, relevant history, prior treatment, and proposed indication.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proposed regimen" icon="fa-regular fa-prescription-bottle-medical" href="/california-off-label/evidence">
    Record dose, frequency, titration, duration, and the clinical reason for the proposed regimen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Care setting" icon="fa-regular fa-hospital" href="/california-off-label/additional-requirements">

  </Card>

## Decision map

<Steps toc={true}>

<Step title="1. Is the proposed use on-label or off-label?">

Compare the proposed indication, population, route, dosage form, and other relevant labeling conditions against the current FDA-approved labeling.

If the proposed use is outside the approved labeling, continue through this workflow. Off-label status is a classification, not by itself a finding of unlawfulness or medical appropriateness.

[Review FDA labeling](/regulatory)

</Step>

<Step title="2. What California requirements actually apply?">

Separate the prescriber's authority, examination and medical-indication requirements, controlled-substance rules, telehealth requirements, patient-specific requirements, facility rules, and program-specific rules.

[California prescribing authority](/california-off-label/prescribing-authority)

</Step>

<Step title="3. What evidence supports the proposed use?">

Build an evidence dossier. Rank evidence by study design and relevance. Preserve important limitations and contradictory findings instead of manufacturing a bibliography-shaped confirmation bias machine.

[Evidence framework](/california-off-label/evidence)

</Step>

<Step title="4. What must be discussed with the patient?">

Identify mandatory disclosures separately from clinically appropriate informed-consent discussion. Flag drug-specific, population-specific, facility-specific, and statutory requirements when they apply.

[Consent framework](/california-off-label/consent)

</Step>

<Step title="5. Can the clinical reasoning be reconstructed?">

Record the medical indication, patient-specific rationale, alternatives considered, expected benefits, material risks, evidence relied upon, monitoring plan, and follow-up criteria.

[Documentation framework](/california-off-label/documentation)

</Step>

<Step title="6. Does coverage require a separate pathway?">

If a health plan, public program, or workers' compensation payer is involved, evaluate coverage and authorization separately from the clinician's prescribing decision.

[Coverage authorization](/coverage)

</Step>

</Steps>

## Evidence dossier

The eventual provider interface should assemble a dossier with four explicit buckets:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Supports" icon="fa-regular fa-circle-check" href="/california-off-label/evidence">
    Studies, guidelines, systematic reviews, regulatory material, and other evidence supporting efficacy or safety.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contradicts or limits" icon="fa-regular fa-triangle-exclamation" href="/california-off-label/evidence">
    Negative studies, safety signals, population limitations, uncertainty, conflicting guidelines, and evidence that weakens the rationale.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patient-specific" icon="fa-regular fa-user" href="/california-off-label/documentation">
    Facts that make the evidence applicable, inapplicable, stronger, weaker, or otherwise relevant to this patient.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Authority provenance" icon="fa-regular fa-scale-balanced" href="/california-off-label/authority-model">
    Exact statute, regulation, agency material, FDA source, guideline, and evidence citation with applicability and verification metadata.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Output

The target output is an auditable prescribing record, not an automatically generated medical decision. The record should preserve the provider's inputs, authorities considered, evidence selected, contradictory evidence reviewed, consent/documentation steps, and monitoring plan.
