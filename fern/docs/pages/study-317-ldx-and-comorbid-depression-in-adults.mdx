**J Clin Psychopharm** (2015)

**Authors:** McElroy et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Significant reduction in depressive symptoms when ADHD is treated concurrently.
