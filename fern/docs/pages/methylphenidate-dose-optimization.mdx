## Methylphenidate dose optimization for ADHD treatment: review of safety, efficacy and tolerance

**Drugs** (2017)

**Authors:** Michael Huss, Ylva Ginsberg, Troels Arngrim

**Medication:** Methylphenidate (MPH-LA)

**Usage:** Off-label (High-dose optimization up to 80mg/day)

**Study Type:** Review and Meta-Analysis

**Country:** Germany / Global

## Key Findings

Doses of 40, 60, and 80 mg were found to be optimal for similar proportions of patients, indicating that high-dose off-label titration is necessary for a significant subset of adults.

## Argument Support

Supports the clinical necessity and safety of dose optimization up to 80mg/day, which exceeds the standard 60mg maximum in many regions.
