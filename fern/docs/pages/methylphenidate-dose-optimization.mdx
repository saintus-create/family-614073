## Key Findings

Doses of 40, 60, and 80 mg were found to be optimal for similar proportions of patients, indicating that high-dose off-label titration is necessary for a significant subset of adults.

## Clinical Question

What proportion of adults require high-dose (up to 80 mg) methylphenidate titration for optimal response?
