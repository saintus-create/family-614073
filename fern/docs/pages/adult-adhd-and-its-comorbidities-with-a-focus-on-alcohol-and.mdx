## Adult ADHD and its comorbidities, with a focus on alcohol and drug use disorders

**Canadian Journal of Psychiatry** (2018)

**Authors:** Sadek J, et al.

**Region:** Canada

**Medication focus:** Stimulants, Non-stimulants

**Dosing strategy:** Boosters and off-label management

## Key Findings

Supports the use of long-acting stimulants but emphasizes the clinical need for IR 'boosters' in the afternoon for adults to cover late-day impairments, a strategy widely adopted in Canadian clinical practice.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30114944/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30114944/)
