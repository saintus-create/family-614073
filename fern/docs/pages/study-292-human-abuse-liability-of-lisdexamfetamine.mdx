## Human Abuse Liability of Lisdexamfetamine

**J Psychopharmacol** (2009)

**Authors:** Jasinski et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 50–150 mg (Oral/IV)

## Key Findings

Delayed Tmax and prodrug kinetics result in significantly lower drug liking scores.
