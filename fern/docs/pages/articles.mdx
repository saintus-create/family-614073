### Article 1 — Pharmacological interventions for ADHD
- **Medication:** Amphetamines, Methylphenidate, Guanfacine, Atomoxetine
- **Usage Type:** Off-label (unlicensed high-dose) and Dose Titration Support
- **Key Findings:** Identified dose-effect patterns across age groups. For amphetamines, efficacy plateaued at ~25mg/day in children and ~50mg/day in adults. Methylphenidate showed no clear plateau in children but peaked at ~50mg/day in adults.
- **Source:** [The Lancet Psychiatry](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpsy/article/PIIS2215-0366(26)00091-X/fulltext)

### Article 2 — Treatment Outcomes With Licensed and Unlicensed Stimulant Doses
- **Medication:** Methylphenidate, Amphetamines
- **Usage Type:** Off-label (unlicensed high-dose)
- **Key Findings:** Meta-analysis found that unlicensed stimulant doses were not associated with greater efficacy than licensed high doses but had a significantly higher risk of discontinuation due to adverse events.
- **Source:** [JAMA Psychiatry](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2811310)
