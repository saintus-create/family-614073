**Journal:** Archives of General Psychiatry (JAMA Psychiatry) (1999)

**Authors:** MTA Cooperative Group

**Medication:** Methylphenidate

**Usage Type:** Multi-unit/day (thrice-daily dosing)

**Study Type:** Randomized Controlled Trial

**Country:** USA

**DOI:** [10.1001/archpsyc.56.12.1073](https://doi.org/10.1001/archpsyc.56.12.1073)

## Key Findings

The intensive medication management (3 doses per day) was superior to behavioral therapy and community care, setting the standard for multi-unit daily dosing.

## Argument Support

This landmark study used an intensive multi-dose (TID) strategy, demonstrating its efficacy compared to standard community care.
