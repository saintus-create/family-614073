**PHARMACOLOGICAL TREATMENT · Evidence Review**

# A 14-Month Randomized Clinical Trial of Treatment Strategies for Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

> **KEY FINDINGS**
>
> The intensive medication management (3 doses per day) was superior to behavioral therapy and community care, setting the standard for multi-unit daily dosing.

**CLINICAL QUESTION**
Is intensive multi-dose methylphenidate management superior to behavioral or community treatment?
