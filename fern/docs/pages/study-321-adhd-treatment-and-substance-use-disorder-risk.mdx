## ADHD Treatment and Substance Use Disorder Risk

**Brit J Psychiatry** (2013)

**Authors:** Groenman et al.

**Country:** Netherlands

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 30–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Longitudinal evidence: Stimulant treatment does not increase, but likely reduces SUD risk.

**DOI/Link:** 23501202
