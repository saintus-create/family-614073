**Journal:** The Journal of Pediatric Pharmacology and Therapeutics (2017)

**Authors:** Shannon G Panther, Alice M Knotts, Tamara Odom-Maryon

**Medication:** Various ADHD medications (Stimulants and Non-stimulants)

**Usage Type:** Off-label (Preschool age 3-5)

**Study Type:** Observational Study / Trends Analysis

**Country:** USA

**DOI:** [10.5863/1551-6776-22.6.423](https://doi.org/10.5863/1551-6776-22.6.423)

## Key Findings

Documented high rates of off-label prescribing in very young children, suggesting clinical reliance on these medications despite a lack of specific FDA approval for this age group.

## Argument Support

Highlights that the majority (91%) of ADHD prescriptions for children aged 3-5 are off-label and provides data on the clinical usage trends.
