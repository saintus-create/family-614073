## Definition

**Polypharmacy** is the clinical situation in which a person uses multiple medications, creating a need to assess medication appropriateness, interactions, duplication, adherence, monitoring, and continuity. The term is a clinical organizing concept; it should not be treated as a standalone CenCal coverage rule or as a conclusion about an individual member.
