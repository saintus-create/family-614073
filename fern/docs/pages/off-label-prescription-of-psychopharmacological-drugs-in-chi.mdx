## Off-Label Prescription of Psychopharmacological Drugs in Child and Adolescent Psychiatry in Denmark

**Journal of Clinical Psychopharmacology** (2016)

**Authors:** Braüner JV, Nielsen ES, Pagsberg AK

**Region:** Denmark, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** ADHD medications, Antipsychotics, Antidepressants

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label use (44% of patients)

## Key Findings

High prevalence of off-label prescribing (44%) in clinical practice. The study highlights that off-label use is common for ADHD medications in younger children (under 6) and for comorbid conditions.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27529772/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27529772/)
