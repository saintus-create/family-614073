## Safety and pharmacokinetics of LDX in Japanese and Caucasian adults

**J Clin Pharmacol** (2010)

**Authors:** Boellner et al.

**Country:** Japan/USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 20–70 mg once daily

## Key Findings

Linear dose proportionality; median Tmax 3–5 hours; mean t½ ~10 hours.

**DOI/Link:** PMC7292221
