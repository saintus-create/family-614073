CenCal Health is the public Medi-Cal managed-care entity serving Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo counties through the County Organized Health System model. Its legal identity, contract authority and operational duties derive from California’s county health-system statutes, the DHCS managed-care contract, applicable All Plan Letters, and CenCal’s published provider materials.

## Legal Status

California Welfare and Institutions Code section 14087.54 authorizes one or more county boards of supervisors to establish a special commission to arrange publicly assisted medical care. The statute treats the commission as a public entity separate from the participating counties and provides that its statutory, contractual and other obligations are obligations of the commission alone, not of the county or the state.

CenCal’s legal entity is the Santa Barbara San Luis Obispo Regional Health Authority, doing business as CenCal Health. CenCal traces its operational origin to the Santa Barbara Health Initiative, which began in 1983, and identifies its expansion into San Luis Obispo County in 2008.

## Operating Role

Within its two-county service area, CenCal functions as the single Medi-Cal managed-care plan under the County Organized Health System structure. DHCS audit materials identify CenCal as operating under DHCS contract authority and reported 241,571 Medi-Cal members as of December 2024.

CenCal is governed by a thirteen-member Board of Directors appointed by the Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo County Boards of Supervisors. CenCal describes the board as including local government representatives, physicians, hospital representatives, member representatives, other health-care provider representatives and business representatives.

## Benefit-Channel Allocation

Medication authorization depends first on the benefit channel. Retail pharmacy claims are administered through Medi-Cal Rx. Physician-administered drugs billed on a medical or institutional claim remain within CenCal’s medical-pharmacy process unless a state Fee-for-Service carve-out applies.

That channel distinction controls the responsible reviewer, the submission route, the governing criteria and the appeal pathway. A pharmacy prior authorization is not the same administrative act as a CenCal medical Treatment Authorization Request.

## Utilization Review and Appeals

CenCal’s utilization-management materials identify Treatment Authorization Requests, provider portal submission, clinical documentation requirements, timelines, deferrals and TAR appeals. DHCS All Plan Letter 21-011 supplies the broader Medi-Cal managed-care framework for authorization decisions, notices, appeals and State Fair Hearings.
