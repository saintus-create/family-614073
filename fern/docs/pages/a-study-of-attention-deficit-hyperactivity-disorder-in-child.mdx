## A study of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in children and adolescents in a tertiary care psychiatric hospital in North India

**Indian Journal of Psychiatry** (2011)

**Authors:** Malhotra S, Patra BN, Subhubuthi S

**Region:** India

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Atomoxetine

**Dosing strategy:** Clinical medication patterns (including IR methylphenidate multiple doses)

## Key Findings

In India, immediate-release methylphenidate is common and often requires multiple daily doses (2-3 times/day) to maintain therapeutic efficacy throughout the day, as long-acting versions were less accessible.
