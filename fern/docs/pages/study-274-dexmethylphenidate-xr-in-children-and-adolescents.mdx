## Dexmethylphenidate XR in children and adolescents

**J Child Adol Psych** (2006)

**Authors:** Findling et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 5–30 mg/day

## Key Findings

Effective symptom control; similar safety profile to racemic MPH with lower active mg.

**DOI/Link:** 10.1089/cap.2006
