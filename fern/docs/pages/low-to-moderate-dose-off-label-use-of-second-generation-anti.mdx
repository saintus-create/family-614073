## Low to moderate-dose off-label use of second-generation antipsychotics in child and adolescent psychiatry

**European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry** (2025)

**Authors:** Kirsi K, Aitkoski K, Kaltiala R

**Region:** Finland, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Quetiapine, Risperidone, Aripiprazole

**Dosing strategy:** Off-label adjunct therapy for ADHD symptoms

## Key Findings

In Finland, 40% of children prescribed off-label low-dose antipsychotics were concurrently taking ADHD medication, using the antipsychotics to manage aggressive behavior or sleep issues associated with ADHD.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40932558/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40932558/)
