## ADHD in adults: clinical characteristics and intensity of symptoms in 1,100 Norwegian patients

**Nordic Journal of Psychiatry** (2010)

**Authors:** Haavik J, Halen B, Landaas ET, et al.

**Region:** Norway, Northern Europe

**Medication focus:** Methylphenidate, Amphetamines

**Dosing strategy:** Adult ADHD dosing

## Key Findings

Detailed the clinical treatment of 1,100 Norwegian adults, showing that many required doses and regimens that were off-label relative to pediatric guidelines, including split doses for better symptom control.

**Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20948775/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20948775/)
