## Long-term safety of Mixed Amphetamine Salts XR in adults

**J Atten Disord** (2008)

**Authors:** Spencer et al.

**Country:** USA

**Language:** English

**Dosing Regimen Studied:** 20–60 mg/day

## Key Findings

24-month safety sustained; no clinically significant cardiovascular changes observed.

**DOI/Link:** 10.1177/10870542
